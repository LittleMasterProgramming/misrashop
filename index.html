Our story

Fashion thatspeaks your language.

MISRA is built on one idea: clothing should say what you want to say, in the language you think in. Classic or bold, festive or everyday.

We choose dresses, shoes, ethnic wear, bags and jewellery for the women of Bangladesh, and make buying them easy: cash on delivery, nationwide delivery, and a virtual try-on so you can see a piece before it arrives.

Founder & face of MISRA

Srabonti Bhattacharjee Swarna

“Our customer’s first-hand experience comes before everything else.”

Srabonti is the heart of MISRA. She leads the brand with one rule: a customer should love a piece from the moment she sees it to the moment she wears it. That means honest photos and descriptions, careful checking before packing, and real people who answer when you ask.

She listens closely to what customers say about fit, fabric and delivery, and turns it into better collections, so every season feels more like you.
